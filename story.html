Our Story

How we found each other

Despite both going to Mizzou together for 4 years, Tatianna and David graduated having never shared any words or interactions. That being said, David undoubtedly admired Tatianna from afar. Coincidentally, they were both in Europe at the same time the summer of 2019 after graduating. David was visiting his nonna in Italy, and Tatianna her yiayia in Greece — both reflecting on their 4 years at Mizzou, enthusiastic and nervous about entering the real world. Little did they know, their lives were soon about to change upon returning to the states.

In October, they found themselves together in St. Louis, out for Mexican food with mutual friends. While Tatianna probably didn't think much of that night, David was taken aback by Tatianna's beauty and personality.

Two days later, David worked up the courage to message Tatianna asking her out on a date — the rest is history.
