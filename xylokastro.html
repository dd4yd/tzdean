Xylokastro Guide

What to do, what to bring, and how to make the most of your stay.

Discover our favorite local experiences, practical travel tips, and must-pack items for a smooth and memorable trip to Xylokastro.

Local recommendations

Stroll the seaside promenade, explore the harbor, and enjoy the charming cafes and tavernas near the hotel. Xylokastro is small but full of character — it’s perfect for relaxed walks, coffee by the sea, and easy evenings with friends.

  • Visit the central beach and harbor
  • Try seafood and mezze at a local taverna
  • Take a short drive to the ancient site of Corinth or the artsy village of Derveni

Best things to do

There’s plenty to enjoy in and around Xylokastro for both quick visits and longer stays.

  • Relax at the beach and swim in the Ionian waters
  • Walk the coastal path toward the marble village of Stymfalia
  • Take a sunset boat ride along the gulf
  • Explore market stalls and local bakeries in town

What to pack

Pack for warm days, cooler evenings, and effortless travel.

  • Light layers, linen, and comfortable walking shoes
  • Swimwear, sun hat, and sunscreen
  • Evening outfits for dinner and wedding events
  • A reusable water bottle and small day bag

Travel essentials

These items will make navigating Greece easier and more comfortable.

  • Passport and travel documents
  • International Driving Permit if you rent a car
  • Power adapter for European outlets
  • Phone charger, portable battery, and travel insurance
