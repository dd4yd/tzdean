Accommodations

Recommended stays for the wedding weekend.

If you’re looking for the perfect place to stay, we recommend Arion Hotel Xylokastro. Over the years, we’ve spent many summers here. Its warm hospitality and unbeatable location makes it an easy favorite.

Located on the seaside, the hotel is just steps from charming cafés, restaurants, bars, and many of our wedding festivities—making it easy to enjoy everything Xylokastro has to offer without needing a car.

Arion Hotel Xylokastro

Tatianna’s favorite thing about the hotel is pulling the blinds open and waking up each morning to the beautiful view of the sea from the private balcony. The hotel also offers a private beach with lounge chairs and food/beverage service, breakfast, and complimentary parking.

We’ve reserved a room block at Arion Hotel Xylokastro for our wedding guests. To receive our special wedding rate, please contact the hotel directly by email and mention Bollwerk & Dean Wedding 2027.

Airbnb

Airbnb is another wonderful option. Xylokastro offers a variety of beautiful homes and apartments, many within walking distance of the beach, restaurants, and our wedding events. It’s a great way to enjoy a little extra space while experiencing the charm of the town like a local.
