Options for the wedding weekend stay.

We recommend booking early, as July is a popular travel month in this part of Greece. Xylokastro is a lovely seaside town, and nearby accommodations can fill quickly around the weekend.

For those who prefer a simpler stay, apartments and short-term rentals are also a great option if you want a little more space or a kitchen for the trip.
