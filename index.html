Travel & Accommodations

Everything you need for a seamless stay.

Xylokastro is a beautiful coastal town with easy access to the airport and a relaxed pace for a summer escape. We recommend arriving the day before the ceremony so you can settle in and enjoy the town.

Recommended stays

  • Hotel Aegean View — 10-minute walk to the town square
  • Seaside Suites — Beachfront, ideal for couples and families
  • Villa Maris — Private terrace and sunset views

Airport: Kalamata Airport, approximately 1.5 hours away.

Parking: Complimentary parking is available at both the ceremony and reception locations.
