Getting Here

How to reach Xylokastro and the wedding weekend.

Xylokastro is a beautiful coastal town with easy access from Athens and a relaxed pace for a summer escape.

For those flying in, Athens Airport is the closest major airport, approximately 1.5 hours away by car. There is also a very good train service from the airport to Kiato, a neighboring town, which makes it convenient to continue onward by taxi or rental car.

If you are planning to drive, the route is straightforward and scenic, and it is a lovely way to arrive at the coast.

Helpful notes

  • Consider arriving a day early if you want a more relaxed start to the weekend.
  • Rental cars and taxis are both practical options for the final leg of the trip.
  • If you need additional help with transport, feel free to reach out to us directly.
